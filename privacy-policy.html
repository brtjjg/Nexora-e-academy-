1. Introduction

Nexora Academy ("Nexora Academy", "Academy", "we", "us" or "our") respects the privacy of students, applicants, contributors, visitors, administrators and other users of our services.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected and processed when you use the Nexora Academy website, applications, learning environment, account systems, course services, contributor systems, payment processes and related features.

By using Nexora Academy, you acknowledge that you have had an opportunity to review this Privacy Policy.

2. Definitions

For this Privacy Policy:

  • Personal information means information that identifies or can reasonably be associated with an individual.
  • Processing includes collecting, storing, using, reviewing, modifying, transmitting, analyzing or deleting information.
  • User means a person accessing or using Nexora Academy.
  • Student means a user participating in a course.
  • Contributor means an authorized person who submits educational content for review or publication.
  • Platform means Nexora Academy's website, applications, dashboards, APIs and related digital services.
  • Service provider means a third party that provides infrastructure, payment, analytics, authentication, communications or other services to Nexora Academy.

3. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through Nexora Academy's website and related digital services.

It applies when you:

  • Visit the Academy website.
  • Create an account.
  • Use Google Sign-In.
  • Enroll in a course.
  • Purchase educational services.
  • Complete assignments or examinations.
  • Receive a certificate.
  • Contact support.
  • Participate in groups or discussions.
  • Submit contributor content.
  • Use other Academy features.

4. Who Is Responsible for Your Information

Nexora Academy is responsible for determining how personal information is processed for the operation of its services, subject to applicable law and any specific arrangements with service providers.

Certain third-party providers may independently act as controllers or processors of information under their own policies and contractual arrangements.

5. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with the Platform.

This may include:

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Phone number.
  • Username.
  • Country.
  • County or general location where voluntarily provided.
  • Profile information.
  • Profile photograph where provided.
  • Course selections.
  • Enrollment information.
  • Learning progress.
  • Attendance information.
  • Assignments and examination records.
  • Certificate information.
  • Documents uploaded by users.
  • Payment and transaction information.
  • Messages and support requests.
  • Technical and device information.
  • Information obtained through authentication providers.

6. Identity and Account Information

When you register for an account, we may collect information required to create and maintain your account.

This may include your name, email address, phone number, username, country and other information you voluntarily provide.

We may use this information to identify your account, communicate with you, provide educational services and maintain accurate academic records.

7. Google Sign-In

Nexora Academy may provide Google Sign-In as an authentication method.

If you choose to sign in using Google, Google may provide Nexora Academy with information associated with the Google account according to the authentication process and permissions applicable to that integration.

Depending on the integration, this may include information such as:

  • Name.
  • Email address.
  • Profile information.
  • Google account identifiers.
  • Authentication-related information.

Nexora Academy uses information obtained through Google Sign-In to authenticate your account, associate your identity with your Academy account and provide relevant services.

Nexora Academy does not control Google's systems or privacy practices. Google's own terms and privacy policies may apply to your use of Google services.

8. Authentication and Security Information

We may process information required to authenticate accounts and maintain account security.

This may include login events, session identifiers, authentication tokens, security logs, account status and information relating to suspected unauthorized access.

Authentication credentials are intended to be handled using appropriate technical safeguards.

9. Educational Information

When you participate in a Nexora Academy course, we may create and maintain an educational record associated with your account.

This may include:

  • Courses enrolled in.
  • Course progress.
  • Lessons completed.
  • Assignments.
  • Examination results.
  • Attendance.
  • Completion status.
  • Certificate status.
  • Academic remarks.
  • Course-related communications.

10. Course and Enrollment Information

We may collect information relating to course enrollment and participation.

This information allows Nexora Academy to provide course access, manage student records, determine eligibility for certificates and provide support.

Course information may remain associated with your account as part of the Academy's educational records.

11. Assignments and Assessment Information

Assignments, examinations, quizzes and other assessments may be recorded and associated with your student account.

We may process:

  • Submitted answers.
  • Scores.
  • Grades.
  • Instructor or administrator remarks where applicable.
  • Submission dates.
  • Assessment status.
  • Attempts.

This information may be used to determine course completion and certificate eligibility.

12. Attendance and Learning Activity

Where a course uses attendance or activity tracking, the Platform may record relevant participation events.

This may include lesson access, attendance records, completion events, timestamps and other learning activity.

These records may be used to administer course requirements and monitor student progress.

13. Certificates and Credentials

When you complete applicable course requirements, Nexora Academy may issue a certificate or other credential.

Certificate records may include:

  • Student name.
  • Course title.
  • Course code.
  • Certificate number.
  • Issue date.
  • Completion status.
  • Grade where applicable.
  • Certificate type.
  • Verification information.

Certificate information may be retained as an academic record.

14. Documents and Uploaded Files

Users may voluntarily upload documents or files for admission, education, verification, assignments, contributor submissions or support purposes.

Users should not upload unnecessary sensitive information.

We may process uploaded documents for the purpose for which they were submitted and for legitimate administrative, security or legal purposes.

Uploaded files may be stored using third-party infrastructure providers where necessary to operate the Platform.

15. Payment and Transaction Information

When you make a payment, Nexora Academy may receive transaction-related information such as:

  • Transaction reference.
  • Payment status.
  • Amount.
  • Currency.
  • Course or service purchased.
  • Payment date.
  • Payment provider.
  • Limited payer information provided by the payment provider.

Where a third-party payment processor handles card or other sensitive payment credentials directly, Nexora Academy does not intend to store complete payment-card credentials such as full card numbers.

16. Contributor Information

Contributors may provide information such as their name, email address, phone number, profile information, course-submission information and communications.

This information may be used to administer the contributor program, review submissions, communicate with contributors and manage published educational content.

17. Messages and Communications

The Platform may provide messaging, announcements, groups, discussions or other communication features.

We may process communications and related metadata where necessary to deliver these services, moderate abuse, provide support, investigate violations or maintain platform security.

Users should not submit passwords, payment credentials or unnecessary highly sensitive information through ordinary messaging channels.

18. Customer Support Information

When you contact Nexora Academy support, we may collect information contained in your communication.

This may include your name, email address, account information, screenshots, transaction references, technical details and the contents of your request.

Support information may be used to investigate and resolve your issue.

  • Browser type.
  • Operating system.
  • Device type.
  • Screen characteristics.
  • Language settings.
  • Referring pages.
  • Pages visited.
  • Approximate timestamps.
  • Error logs.
  • Security events.

20. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Nexora Academy may use cookies, local storage, session storage, authentication tokens and similar technologies.

These technologies may be used to:

  • Keep users signed in.
  • Maintain sessions.
  • Remember preferences.
  • Protect accounts.
  • Measure Platform performance.
  • Understand how users interact with the Platform.

You may control certain browser-based technologies through your browser settings, although disabling essential technologies may affect Platform functionality.

21. Analytics

Nexora Academy may use analytics services to understand how the Platform is used and improve its functionality.

Analytics information may include pages visited, approximate geographic information, device information, traffic sources, session information and interactions with the Platform.

Analytics providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

22. Location Information

Nexora Academy may collect general location information provided by users, such as country or county.

Nexora Academy does not need precise GPS location to provide ordinary course services unless a particular feature specifically requires it and appropriate notice is provided.
